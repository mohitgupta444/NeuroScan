About this build. Ground-truth tumor masks were not available in the source dataset (classification-only labels), so U-Net was trained on pseudo-masks derived from the CNN's Grad-CAM attention maps, refined with Otsu thresholding and contour cleanup. Treat segmentation area/location as an approximate research signal, not a clinical measurement. This tool is a research prototype and is not a diagnostic device.
